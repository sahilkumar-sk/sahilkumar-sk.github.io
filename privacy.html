Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Why It's Trending" to upload that channel's own videos.

What data is used

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and their titles and descriptions to the owner's channel. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data from any other person is collected, stored, sold or shared.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
